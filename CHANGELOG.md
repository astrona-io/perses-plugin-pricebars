## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- `PriceBarChart` panel: one bar per category label value, coloured by band against the average of
  the shown bars, a dashed average line that ignores the mouse, the current hour drawn darker on a
  background column, a hover column and a tooltip with the value, its band, the average and how
  much the hour saves or costs against the average ("−25 % vs average, saves 25 %").
- Panel editor, CUE schema with valid and invalid examples.
